Gesture Synth Guide

Gesture Synth is an interactive musical instrument created by Eric Wei. Use your hands to create chords, melodies, and sounds in real-time!

Left Hand

Tilt
Inward → Major
Outward → Minor

Fingers (Scale Degree)
1 → I
2 → II
3 → III
4 → IV
5 → V
Index + Pinky → VI
Index + Pinky + Thumb → VII

Right Hand

Fingers (Chord Quality)
1 → Root Position
2 → 1st Inversion
3 → Major/Minor 7th
4 → Dominant/Diminished 7th

Octave
Thumb In → higher octave
Thumb Out → lower octave

Tilt
Inward → More Filter
Outward → Less Filter

Height
Higher → Louder
Lower → Softer

Melody Mode

The left hand chooses the scale degree. Open one or more fingers on the right hand to play the note and close your fist to release it. Close and reopen to repeat the same note.

Loop Station

Press REC, wait for the one-bar count-in and perform. On the first take, press STOP to close the loop at the next bar. Every later recording becomes an independent track that can be muted, soloed, retuned or deleted.
